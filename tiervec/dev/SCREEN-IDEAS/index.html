    Screen ideas¶

    Experimental. The API is not stable and nothing here is wired into a database engine yet. What may change.

    Every idea for the index is listed here before its first run, with its source, what it is expected to do, and the bar it has to pass. The result is added after the run. An idea that fails stays in the list with its numbers, so that it is not tried twice.

    The ideas are tested in two stages (plan on #159): many quick rounds on the screen, at small sizes, and then a confirmation at larger sizes only for the ideas that pass, under rules written before that run. A screen has no timing, and its sizes and data are small and generated, so no default changes on a screen result alone.

    The data of the screen¶

    The first run used 128 dimensions with noise in all of them and queries near stored rows. It ran the exact and tier arms only, and their lowest recall was 0.999 or more at every size, so a difference between two designs had no room to show.

    Harder data is chosen once, by this rule, written before any run on it. Only the baseline arms run while it is chosen (exact, tier, and graph-a10, the live graph at alpha 1.0), never an idea.

    • The candidates are 768 dimensions with --latent 32, 64, 128, 256, and 768, in this order (the two kinds of data).
    • The choice is the first candidate at which, on a10k with seed 1 and the write mix, recallLowest of both tier and graph-a10 is at most 0.97. If none is, the choice is 768.
    • The chosen data, its difficulty (the DATA lines), and the runs of every candidate that was tried are recorded here.

    Step 1: dimensions that the noise spans¶

    All five candidates ran on a10k, seed 1, on commit d84f2c6 (#175), with 0 violations. Intrinsic dimension and contrast are the DATA line.

    --latent Intrinsic dimension Contrast recallLowest of tier recallLowest of graph-a10 Run
    32 16.5 2.036 1.0000 0.9990 46042784692
    64 24.3 1.868 1.0000 0.9980 46042789843
    128 34.6 1.766 0.9990 0.9960 46042794754
    256 47.2 1.710 0.9980 0.9870 46042798887
    768 91.3 1.631 0.9970 0.9850 46042803864

    No candidate reached the threshold, so by the rule --latent is 768. That still leaves the baselines little room, so a second step follows.

    Step 2: clusters, written before any run of it¶

    • At 768 dimensions with --latent 768, the clusters of a collection (--clusters, 16 on a10k so far) take 8, 4, and 2, in this order: with fewer clusters, more rows are near each query. 2 is the least that lets the distribution move.
    • Only baselines run: exact, tier, graph-a10, and tier-m1 (tier maxSegments=1, where every seal rebuilds all rows into one graph segment: the arm that idea S2 will be compared with).
    • The choice is the first candidate at which, on a10k with seed 1 and the write mix, recallLowest of both graph-a10 and tier-m1 is at most 0.97. If none is, the choice is 2. The default tier is reported and does not decide: it searches several segments with a full beam each, and in step 1 at --latent 768 it computed 6.3 times the distances per search of graph-a10.

    All three ran on a10k, seed 1, on commit d342987 (#176), with 0 violations.

    --clusters Intrinsic dimension Contrast recallLowest of graph-a10 recallLowest of tier-m1 recallLowest of tier Run
    8 103.4 1.599 0.9530 0.9530 0.9980 46045542559
    4 114.1 1.507 0.8560 0.8560 0.9950 46045546229
    2 124.0 1.315 0.7750 0.7750 0.9850 46045549908

    The data of the screen from now on: 768 dimensions, --latent 768, --clusters 8, the first candidate of step 2 that met the threshold. On a10k with seed 1 its queries have an intrinsic dimension of 103.4 and a relative contrast of 1.599. It is harder than real text embeddings are likely to be, so it stretches the designs and says little about the recall a host would see. The default tier stays close to 1 on it. The other shapes, with seed 1 on the same data settings: intrinsic dimension 9.8 and contrast 0.935 on p20 (from 19 neighbours), 7.4 and 1.517 on p200, and 68.1 and 1.571 on p2000.

    The lowest recall of graph-a10 and tier-m1 is at the first checkpoint, right after the load, which puts all rows into half of the clusters. A bar on recallLowest would so judge the load and not the churn. results.tsv has a new column, recallChurnMean, the mean recall of the ten checkpoints after the load, and the bars below use it.

    How to add an idea¶

    1. Add a row below with its source, hypothesis, and pass bar, and the status not run. The bar names a shape, a column of results.tsv, and a number.
    2. Add its variants to src/bench/agent-memory-screen.variants, or give them to the run. A change in the library is tried on its branch, as a new setting or a new arm.
    3. Commit, then run the screen on that commit: gh workflow run screen.yml --ref <branch>.
    4. Write the result and the link to the run into the row, and set the status to passed or failed. Change a bar only before the first run.

    Ideas¶

    Id Idea and source Hypothesis Pass bar Status and result
    S1 A live graph with alpha 1.2. FreshDiskANN reports stable recall under churn with its repair rules for the values of alpha it tried except 1; #161 Over three replacements of the live rows, alpha 1.2 keeps recall better than alpha 1.0 On a10k, with each of the seeds 1, 2, and 3: recallChurnMean of graph-a12 is at least 0.005 above that of graph-a10, with at most 1.25 times its distancesPerSearch failed: recallChurnMean 0.023 to 0.059 lower with alpha 1.2 (results)
    S2 Merge by insertion into the largest segment, MergeMode.INSERT (#16, #161) It keeps the recall of a rebuild and writes cost less graph work On a10k, with each of the seeds 1, 2, and 3: recallChurnMean of tier-m1-insert is at most 0.005 below that of tier-m1, with at most 0.7 times its distancesPerRowWritten failed: the recall bar was missed with seed 1 (0.0103 lower); the cost bar held with every seed (results)
    S3 A live graph with chunks of 64 slots. On the first data, at 20 rows, the graph held about four times the bytes per row of the tier. The cause in the code: GraphIndex allocates a whole chunk of 1,024 slots (CHUNK_SHIFT 10) for its first node, with vectors, ids, link references, states, and in-degrees for every slot, and bytesHeld counts it all. At 768 dimensions one chunk is 3,166,224 bytes, or 158,311 bytes for each of 20 rows before any link list The chunk is why a small live graph takes so much memory, and chunks of 64 slots remove most of it without changing the graph With seed 1: bytesHeldPerRow of graph-a10 with CHUNK_SHIFT 6 is at most 1.5 times that of tier on p20 and on p200, and on every shape of the screen its recallChurnMean, distancesPerSearch, and distancesPerRowWritten equal those of a run with CHUNK_SHIFT 10 that reports the same vector lanes passed: 0.30 and 0.23 times the tier on p20 and p200, the other columns equal (results); not merged

    The bars of S1 and S2 were changed on 2026-11-28, before their first run on a10k. They named recallLowest (see above), one seed, and for S2 the default tier. At the default maxSegments of 8 the count rule merges the smallest segments, so by the code the largest one, which MergeMode.INSERT needs, is not among them under churn. S2 is so judged with maxSegments 1, where every merge takes the largest segment; tier and tier-insert still run, to show what the default layout does.

    Results¶

    All runs are the screen preset on its data above, and none has a violation. Nothing is timed. Every variant and shape ran on commit 9754ef8 (#177), the commit with the bars, once for each of the seeds 1, 2, and 3.

    S1 results¶

    alpha 1.2 failed: with every seed it lost recall. On a10k:

    Seed recallChurnMean graph-a10 recallChurnMean graph-a12 Difference (needs +0.005 or more) distancesPerSearch ratio (at most 1.25) Holds Run
    1 0.9747 0.9176 -0.0571 0.828 no 46047276298
    2 0.9741 0.9508 -0.0233 0.839 no 46047279946
    3 0.9733 0.9140 -0.0593 0.820 no 46047284166

    It also computed 2.0 to 2.1 times the distances per written row. The gap was larger on p2000: recallChurnMean 0.7694, 0.7565, and 0.7653 for graph-a12 against 0.9994, 0.9994, and 0.9997 for graph-a10 with the seeds 1, 2, and 3.

    A possible cause, read from the code and not measured: GraphIndex drops a candidate neighbour only when alpha times its distance to a kept neighbour is at most its distance to the node. If most candidates are about as far from each other as from the node, alpha 1.2 drops few of them, and a list fills with the nearest candidates, without the spread that leads a search across the graph. No data with a lower intrinsic dimension at these sizes was run. The result is about alpha in this graph, which does not have the repair rules of FreshDiskANN. It belongs to #161.

    Insertion cost 0.52 to 0.53 times the distances of a rebuild per written row, so the cost bar held. The recall bar failed with seed 1, so the idea failed. With all three seeds insertion ended below the rebuild, by 0.0033 to 0.0103.

    At the default maxSegments of 8, tier-insert gave the same number as tier in every column but the heap, and the same curve, on every shape and seed: MergeMode.INSERT changed nothing there.

    S3 results¶

An earlier run of 570cfcc landed on 16 lanes (46047308966) and is not used for the bar; it held the same bytes per row.

    On every shape recallChurnMean, distancesPerSearch, distancesPerRowWritten, and the recall curve of graph-a10 were equal in the two runs, so the bar was passed. The factor of about four is a matter of small collections: at 200 rows the graph with 1,024 slots already held less than the tier. A pass here changes no default. The screen runs none of the library's tests and times nothing, so the change needs both before it can ship.
